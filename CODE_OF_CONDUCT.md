# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help.

## What that means here

Examples of behaviour that makes this project work:

- Giving feedback on the code, not the person who wrote it.
- Accepting that a maintainer may say no, and that a contributor may disagree.
- Taking responsibility when you get something wrong, and moving on.

Examples of behaviour that is not welcome:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks — public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests or discussions.

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, and anywhere else
someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainer ([@gowtham012](https://github.com/gowtham012))
by direct message or by opening a private security advisory on the
[Security tab](https://github.com/gowtham012/pinpoint/security) if you would
rather it stayed out of a public issue. Reports are handled privately.

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete contributions that break these rules, and
may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for behaviour they consider
inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
